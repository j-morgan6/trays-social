# Frequently Asked Questions

Short, plain-language answers to the things people ask most. For the formal
details, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), [Terms of Service](/terms), and
[Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## What is Trays?

Trays is a recipe-sharing community for home cooks. You post recipes — a photo,
the ingredients, the steps, and the tools you used — and they appear in the
feeds of the cooks who follow you, newest first. You can like, comment, follow
other cooks, and save recipes to your tray to cook later. There is no algorithm
deciding what you see; you see the cooks you chose to follow.

## Who is it for?

Home cooks — anyone who likes making food and wants to share what they cook or
discover what other people are actually making. You do not need to be a
professional or a food blogger. Trays is rated 13+.

## Is it free?

Yes. Posting recipes, following cooks, reading your feed, searching, and saving
to your tray are all free, and recipes are never put behind a paywall. We plan
one optional subscription, **Trays Plus**, which will add kitchen tools on top
of the free app. It is not available to buy yet — see
[What is Trays Plus?](#what-is-trays-plus) below. You will never have to
subscribe to use Trays.

## Do you have ads?

Trays may show clearly labeled sponsored placements. In the app, they can appear
in your feed, in Find, and on the screen you see when you finish cooking a
recipe — never inside a recipe's ingredients or steps, never in your tray, and
never in a way that reorders your feed. You still see the cooks you follow,
newest first. On the website, sponsored placements may appear on public recipe
pages, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

We do not use advertising cookies or ad tracking, and there is no App Tracking
Transparency prompt. Once it is available, Trays Plus will remove sponsored
placements from the app.

## What is Trays Plus?

Trays Plus is an optional subscription coming in a future update of the iOS
app. It is not available to buy yet. When it arrives, it will bundle the kitchen
tools we build around the free app:

- **Custom collections** — organize your saved recipes onto your own shelves.
- **Weekly meal planner** — plan a week of meals from recipes you have saved.
- **Automatic grocery list** — a plain checklist built from the week you
  planned. It is a checklist, not a store: nothing is for sale in it.
- **No ads, anywhere** — sponsored placements are removed throughout the app.

Trays Plus never gates recipes. Every recipe on Trays is free to read, cook,
save, and share whether or not you subscribe.

## What does Trays Plus cost?

Nothing yet: Trays Plus is not available to buy, so there is nothing to pay
today. When it launches, it will be sold through the App Store, and the exact
price in your local currency — and whether a free trial is included — will be
shown before you confirm the purchase. That displayed price is what you pay.
Subscriptions will renew automatically until you cancel. We will update this
answer with the price when Plus launches.

## How do I restore a purchase?

Once Trays Plus is available, if you reinstall Trays or sign in on a new
device, open **Settings**, choose **Trays Plus**, and tap **Restore purchases**.
Your subscription belongs to the Apple ID that bought it, so restoring works on
any device signed in to that Apple ID.

## How do I cancel Trays Plus?

Once Trays Plus is available, you will cancel through Apple, not inside Trays:
open the iOS **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, then
**Trays Plus**, then **Cancel Subscription**. If a free trial is offered,
cancelling during it means you are not charged.

When a subscription ends, nothing you made is deleted. Your collections and
planned weeks stay readable, and you can still tick off and remove items — the
Plus tools simply stop accepting new additions until you subscribe again. Refund
requests are handled by Apple, as described in our
[Terms of Service](/terms).

## Do you track me?

No. We do not run third-party analytics, we do not collect advertising
identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites — so there
is no App Tracking Transparency prompt. We collect only what is needed to run
the service (your account info, the content you post, and basic server logs).
The full breakdown is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## How do I report a problem?

If a post or a cook breaks our [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines),
use the report option on the post, or block the user to stop seeing their
content and hide yours from them. For anything else — a bug, a safety concern,
or a question — email us at [support@trays.app](mailto:support@trays.app) and we
will look into it.

## How do I delete my account?

Open **Settings** and choose **Delete account**. This permanently removes your
account and your content. Deletion is honored within 30 days, and backups are
purged within 90 days, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). If you
have trouble, email [support@trays.app](mailto:support@trays.app).

Deleting your account does not cancel a Trays Plus subscription — cancel that
through Apple as described above, or it will keep renewing.

## Where does my data live?

Your account data, posts, and comments are stored in a Postgres database hosted
by Fly.io in Toronto, Canada. Recipe photos are stored with Tigris Data. We do
not sell your personal information. Note that posts, recipes, comments, and
profiles are public by default and can be viewed on the web — see
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) §7 for exactly what is public versus private.

## How do I contact support?

Email us at [support@trays.app](mailto:support@trays.app). We read every
message.
